Detailed Description

Follows the hard PDDL3 constraints of a domain and its problem while a plan runs.

It is told each state (update) and the time (tick), in seconds since the plan started, and says which constraint is violated as soon as it is; at the end of the plan (finish), those that needed something that did not happen. Preferences are left out: they count only for the planner. A forall is grounded over the objects of its types.
