Detailed Description

The initial task network of a hierarchical (HDDL) problem: what is to be done, as tasks, as the goal is for PDDL.

Inherits from plansys2_msgs::msg::TaskNetwork. Made from an HDDL ":htn" section, or a task at a time:

plansys2::TaskNetwork network("(:htn :ordered-subtasks (and (build car_1) (build car_2)))");
plansys2::TaskNetwork tasks;
auto first = tasks.add("(patrol_wp r2d2 wp1)");
tasks.add("(patrol_wp r2d2 wp2)", first); // after the first
