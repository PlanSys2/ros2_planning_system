Detailed Description

The behavior tree of a hierarchical (HTN) plan, as HDDL executes it.

The tree is the decomposition of the plan (Plan.tasks), not a graph of its actions:

  • every task is a node, named after the task and its method, that succeeds when all of its subtasks have, and fails, and fails those above it, when one of them fails;
  • the precondition of its method is checked (CheckMethodPrecondition) when the plan applied it (PlanTask.applied: once the actions that end by then have ended), and before its subtasks run: if the world is no longer as when the plan was made, the task fails there, before any of its actions start;
  • the subtasks of a method run in the order the method gives them: one after another where it orders them all, at once where it does not;
  • the actions are the leaves (the action template of the executor), each waiting for those that end before it starts in the plan: what the order of the methods leaves open is done as the planner found it, at once where the plan has times that overlap.

It needs a plan with its decomposition (an HDDL planner, such as Klimax), and the executor uses it in HDDL mode only.
