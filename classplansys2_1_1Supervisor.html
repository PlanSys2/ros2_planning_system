Detailed Description

Watches the components once they are up, and acts when one needs it.

Each step asks the state of each component. One that comes back unconfigured (its process started again) is configured and activated. One that does not answer for lost_after is lost; lost for longer than grace, the executor is deactivated, and the plans in course fail with a clear result instead of waiting for it. When every component is active again, so is the executor. A component deactivated by someone else is left as it is.
