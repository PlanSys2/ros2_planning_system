Detailed Description

Whether a component of PlanSys2 is active, as its clients wait for it.

A call of a PlanSys2 client waits until its component is active, however long it takes while ROS runs, and goes on as soon as it is: an application may start before PlanSys2. Once seen active it is not asked again, until one of its services goes or stops answering.
